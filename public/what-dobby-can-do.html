User Playbook & Capabilities

🪄 What Dobby Can Do

Your complete operational guide for Dobby — the AI Elf assistant for family commitments, school circulars, utility bills, and proactive WhatsApp reminders.

✨ 1. AI Task & Reminder Extraction

Convert chaotic text messages, photos, PDFs, and invoices into structured calendar tasks with zero manual typing.

Cognitive Engine

💡 What It Does

WhatsApp Group Chat Ingestion Reads chat logs where family members make casual promises (e.g. "I'll pick up Maya from soccer at 4:30 PM tomorrow") and isolates actionable tasks.
Bills & Due Date Extraction Inspects utility bills, electric invoices, or credit card slips in PNG/JPEG/PDF format to identify due date, total amount, and account number.
School & Doctor Notices Parses school calendars, permission slips, and doctor follow-up slips with multiple deadlines and converts each one to a separate task.
Temporal Grounding Resolves relative phrases like "tomorrow at 3 PM" or "next Tuesday" into exact calendar dates (YYYY-MM-DD) based on your local timezone.

📋 How to Perform This Task

1
Locate the AI Ingest Engine card at the top of the Dobby home dashboard.
2
Provide your messy data in any format:
  • Type or paste text directly into the text box.
  • Drag & drop files (PNG, JPG, PDF, Word .docx, CSV, TXT) into the upload basin.
  • Paste a screenshot directly from your clipboard using Ctrl+V / Cmd+V.
  • Or click any of the 4 ready-to-test sample presets (WhatsApp Chat, SMS Alert, Utility Bill, or School Circular).
3
Click the blue "Extract Tasks with Dobby" button. The cognitive engine parses your content and extracts candidates with confidence ratings and original source quotes.
4
Review the suggestions, fine-tune the title, due date, priority, or assignee if desired, then click "Commit All Tasks" to save them directly to the database.
✅ 2. Family Task Organization & Tracking

Centralized dashboard for tracking pending, overdue, and completed household commitments.

Household Workflow

💡 What It Does

  • Organizes household duties into Today, Upcoming, Overdue, and Completed views.
  • Assigns tasks to specific family members (Mom, Dad, Kids, Roommates) so everyone knows their responsibilities.
  • Displays AI extraction confidence ratings and the exact excerpt quote where the task was discovered.
  • Supports four priority levels: Urgent, High, Medium, and Low.

📋 How to Perform This Task

1
Click the Tasks tab in the navigation bar.
2
Use the filter pills (All, Pending, Completed, Today) or filter by assigned family member to narrow down duties.
3
To add a task manually, click "+ New Task", enter a title, choose a due date/time, select priority, and click Save.
4
Click the round checkbox on any task to mark it completed. Completed items can be uncompleted at any time.
⏰ 3. Timed Reminders & Snooze Scheduling

Background daemon running every 60 seconds to notify you right when commitments are due.

Automated Daemon

💡 What It Does

  • Background server daemon queries due reminders every 60 seconds and atomically triggers them.
  • Provides intelligent timing presets: Exact Due Time, 1 Hour Before, 2 Hours Before, or 1 Day Before.
  • Instant snooze capabilities (10-minute snooze, 1-hour snooze, or tomorrow morning).
  • Creates in-app notification bell alerts and dispatches external WhatsApp pings.

📋 How to Perform This Task

1
On any task card, click the clock icon or "Set Reminder".
2
Select a preset (e.g. 1 Hour Before) or pick an exact reminder date and time.
3
Switch to the Reminders tab to view all active, triggered, and snoozed alerts.
4
Click "Trigger Test Now" on any scheduled reminder to test the alert pipeline immediately.
📱 4. WhatsApp & Mobile Phone Alerts

Receive proactive pings on your smartphone when important household deadlines arrive.

Direct Messaging

💡 What It Does

  • Dispatches deadline reminders to your personal phone number via WhatsApp.
  • Supports custom HTTP Webhooks (compatible with Twilio, Zapier, Make.com, n8n).
  • Maintains a delivery audit log tracking sent and acknowledged messages.

📋 How to Perform This Task

1
Click the green "WhatsApp" button in the top navigation bar.
2
Enter your phone number with the country code (e.g. +91 9876543210 or +1 4155552671).
3
Toggle WhatsApp alerts ON and click "Save Preferences".
4
Click "Send Test Alert" to verify that your smartphone receives the alert ping.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 5. Household Circles & Family Collaboration

Share household responsibilities with partners, children, and roommates.

Collaboration

💡 What It Does

  • Create separate circles (e.g. "Rivera Household", "Vacation Planning", "Kid Duties").
  • Invite family members via unique invite codes.
  • Interactive Family Message Board to discuss tasks and turn chat promises into active duties.

📋 How to Perform This Task

1
Click the Family & Chat tab in the navigation bar.
2
Create a new group or select an existing household circle.
3
Copy the unique invite code to share with other family members so they can join the circle.
